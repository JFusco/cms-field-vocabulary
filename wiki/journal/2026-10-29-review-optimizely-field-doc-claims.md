# Review Optimizely field-doc claims

[Issue #100](https://github.com/JFusco/cms-field-vocabulary/issues/100) asked for an official-source claim comparison for the eleven Optimizely field guides whose October 18 fragment fingerprints classified `claim-changing`. On October 29 a full scan reached all eleven redirected `docs.optimizely.com` pages with no missing required or observed tokens. Two consecutive fetches returned identical fragment fingerprints for every page. The CMS SaaS, CMS 12 built-in, and CMS 12 attribute pages still matched their October 18 fingerprints. The eight CMS 13 pages had changed again: the October 26 daily scan already differed for the built-in and property-access pages, and October 29 differed for all eight. That includes the Content property page reviewed in [issue #107](https://github.com/JFusco/cms-field-vocabulary/issues/107). The pages are Archbee-rendered, and each fragment hash covers markup, image URLs, and block IDs. A changed hash therefore triggers this text review but is not evidence of a field change.

Each registered locator was compared with every profile claim that cites the source. The table records the October 18 observation, the reviewed October 29 observation, and the disposition. "Locked" means `pnpm sources:review` wrote that exact reviewed fingerprint to `sources.lock.json`. "Held" means the lock keeps its earlier reviewed observation until a focused profile PR resolves the listed claim.

The CMS 13 global-contract and external-mapping applicability decision is still the one recorded for issue #107. A global contract is a Content Reference picker and Graph behavior. Mapped external properties are read-only authoring behavior, not native property labels. The mapping section now also lists relaxed source-to-target compatibilities, late-addition read-only behavior, one-level block depth, and reversible direction. None of these adds a native field token.

Release impact: the eight locked observations change only `sources.lock.json` and the regenerated catalog source digests. No profile, field identity, generated platform document, or consumer route changes. The three held observations need focused profile PRs with exact claim evidence before their source-lock entries advance. Issue #100 stays open until those PRs merge or a maintainer records a no-change decision. The scanner will keep reporting the held sources, and it may report CMS 13 markup churn again before the next review.
